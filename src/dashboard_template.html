Trickle-Down Trend Tracker

Do runway trends really move from luxury houses to semi-luxury brands to the high street? This tracks the Fall/Winter 2026 trends through four brand tiers using weekly news coverage, then tests whether a retailer could use that press as an early demand signal.

Press data: GDELT article counts only (no article text), normalised by each tier's own press volume. Retail data: synthetic, for a fictional retailer.
